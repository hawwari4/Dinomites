# QSTP Internship Program: Operational Knowledge Base

## 1. Program Structure & Logistics
* **Duration:** 12 weeks per cycle[cite: 6].
* **Format:** In-person at Qatar Science & Technology Park[cite: 6].
* **Commitment Options:** 
  * Part-time (20 hours/week)[cite: 6].
  * Full-time (40 hours/week)[cite: 6].
* **Flexibility:** Scheduling is flexible and structured to complement academic schedules based on startup needs[cite: 6].

## 2. Cohort Timelines (3 Cycles Per Year)
* **Winter Cycle:** 29 January – 21 April[cite: 6].
* **Summer Cycle:** 14 June – 3 September[cite: 6].
* **Fall Cycle:** 1 October – 20 December[cite: 6].

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
* **Academic Status:** Current seniors or recent graduates (graduated within the past two years)[cite: 6].
* **Location:** Must be enrolled at a local university in Qatar[cite: 6].
* **Profile:** Must possess relevant skills or an interest in technology, innovation, or entrepreneurship[cite: 6].

## 4. Official Application Workflow
The process follows a strict 5-step pipeline[cite: 6]:
1. Startups identify intern requirements[cite: 6].
2. Talents complete the online application[cite: 6].
3. Matching process occurs between talents and startups[cite: 6].
4. Startups interview selected candidates[cite: 6].
5. QSTP onboards the selected interns[cite: 6].
